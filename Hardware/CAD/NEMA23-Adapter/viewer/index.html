Tercio S1 · Accessory

NEMA 23 adapter plate

A compact cap that replaces the S1's NEMA 17 cover. It follows the outline of the motor's rear end cap, so nothing sticks out past the motor. The board drops into a pocket and screws onto bosses at its own holes, which puts the encoder exactly on the motor axis. Four M3 screws hold the plate in the motor's own rear screw holes.

Key dimensions

Kit

  • Adapter plate: 6061-T6 aluminium, bead-blasted or black anodised (PETG / PA-CF printable)
  • Magnet: Ø6 × 2.5 mm, diametric, N35, glued to the shaft end
  • 4 × M3 × 10 button head (plate → motor rear holes)
  • 4 × M3 × 6 socket head (S1 → plate)

Assembly

  1. Clean the rear shaft end and glue the magnet centred on it (cyanoacrylate or epoxy).
  2. Take the S1 off its NEMA 17 cover and screw it into the plate with 4 × M3 × 6.
  3. Set the plate on the motor's rear face with the J5 channel on the motor's lead side.
  4. Screw it down with 4 × M3 × 10 into the motor's rear screw holes.

Notes

The motor follows the supplied 57 mm NEMA 23 drawing: 56.4 max frame, 47.14 ± 0.2 mm holes Ø5.1, pilot Ø38.1 × 1.6, shaft Ø8, 5 mm flange, 56 mm body, shaft end flush at the rear. The rear screw pattern (47.14 × 29.0 mm, M3) differs between makers: measure your motor before cutting metal. It is one line in adapter.py.
